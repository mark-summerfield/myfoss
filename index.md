For over a decade (2014-2024) we have sold PDF comparison products that were both accurate and very fast through our company, [Qtrac Ltd](https://www.qtrac.eu).
Unfortunately, we have been victims of the Digital River/MyCommerce scandal (see, e.g., [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2024/10/15/digital_river_runs_dry_hasnt/)), and our company has now ceased trading.

*In view of the EU’s Cyber Resilience Act and an abundance of caution, we
have withdrawn all our free software (see [EU CRA](europe.md)).*

So we no longer provide any software, either commercial or free open source, although we continue to write it.
